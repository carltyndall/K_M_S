ª# Learning loop

1. **Capture:** Copy the [daily template](../templates/daily-note.md) into [daily notes](../active_learning/daily_notes/README.md). Record the topic, source and date, and explain it in your own words.
2. **Practice:** Predict a result, try a small example, and record what happened. Keep useful experiments in [experiments](../active_learning/experiments/README.md).
3. **Review:** Answer your recall question without looking, check the answer, and revisit it periodically (for example, tomorrow and next week). Practice again if it is unclear.
4. **Promote:** Copy the [knowledge template](../templates/knowledge-note.md) when an insight will be useful again. Put a general idea in [concepts](../concepts/README.md), or tool-specific details in [software](../software/README.md); link back to its source and daily note.
5. **Retrieve and apply:** Find the note when solving a new problem, recall the idea, and adapt it. Record what you learn in a new daily note and improve the reusable note.
6. **Keep current:** Record dates and relevant versions. Recheck version-sensitive claims against current documentation before relying on them and update notes as needed.

AI assistance is optional: treat its answers as suggestions, check them against reliable documentation or a small test, and record how you verified them. Avoid sharing private information.

Follow the [worked example](../active_learning/daily_notes/2026-11-24-daily-notes.md) through its practice and promoted notes.
