# Topic

## In my own words
Explain the idea and when it is useful.

## Example and application
Give a small example and link a practice experiment or describe where to apply it.

## Recall and review
Write a question to answer without looking. Record review dates and update anything you misunderstood.

## Sources and context
Link the originating daily note and reliable sources; record dates accessed and relevant software versions. Recheck version-sensitive details when using them later.

## Optional AI assistance
Record any AI suggestion and how you verified it using a reliable source or a small test. Do not include private information.
